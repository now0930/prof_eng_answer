# Eight bounded technical source checks — 2026-11-01

All judgments are limited to the **named fact-id proposition** and are not full Topic Pack or licensed-standard verification. Official OPC Foundation HTML and Emerson handbook indexed text were compared. No complete Emerson PDF image inspection is claimed.

1. **historian_quality_code_severity_and_interoperability_mapping** — KEEP_BOUNDED. Source: OPC UA Part 4 7.11.5/7.38 https://reference.opcfoundation.org/specs/OPC-10000-4/full. Boundary: Good/Uncertain/Bad severity and code semantics are OPC UA specific; third-party mappings require explicit conventions.
2. **historian_data_completeness_expected_observations_and_gaps** — KEEP_BOUNDED. Source: OPC UA Part 11 6.2.2/6.5.3.2 https://reference.opcfoundation.org/specs/OPC-10000-11/6.5.3.2. Boundary: Missing history can be reported as Bad_NoData or Bad_DataLost; an expected-observation completeness denominator is system-defined, not mandated by OPC.
3. **process_historian_stores_retrievable_time_series_with_quality_and_time_semantics** — KEEP_BOUNDED. Source: OPC UA Part 11 4.6 and 6.5.3.2 https://reference.opcfoundation.org/specs/OPC-10000-11/4.6. Boundary: Historic retrieval supports values, timestamps and status; individual historians differ in supported timestamps and retention.
4. **cage_guides_plug_and_enables_flow_characterization** — KEEP_BOUNDED. Source: Emerson Control Valve Handbook chapter 3 https://www.emerson.com/is/content/emerson/ko/final-control/flow-controls/documents/d101881x012.pdf. Boundary: Only cage-guided trim of applicable globe valves; not all valve bodies employ a cage.
5. **reduced_capacity_trim_adapts_valve_flow_capacity** — KEEP_BOUNDED. Source: Emerson Control Valve Handbook chapter 3 Fig 3.42 https://www.emerson.com/is/content/emerson/ko/final-control/flow-controls/documents/d101881x012.pdf. Boundary: Smaller plug/cage/seat combinations or adapted trim reduce rated flow capacity; sizing must remain application-specific.
6. **spring_diaphragm_actuator_simple_reliable_pneumatic_option** — KEEP_BOUNDED. Source: Emerson Control Valve Handbook chapter 3 section 3.8.1 https://www.emerson.com/is/content/emerson/ko/final-control/flow-controls/documents/d101881x012.pdf. Boundary: Document identifies simplicity/dependability, not universal superiority or suitability for high-force cases.
7. **control_valve_actuator_output_matches_valve_motion** — KEEP_BOUNDED. Source: Emerson Control Valve Handbook chapter 3 section 3.8 https://www.emerson.com/is/content/emerson/ko/final-control/flow-controls/documents/d101881x012.pdf. Boundary: Linear/rotary output mechanical compatibility is necessary; conversion linkages and adapters are possible.
8. **gas_required_coefficient_differs_from_published_rated_capacity** — KEEP_BOUNDED. Source: Emerson Valve Sizing chapter and IEC method overview https://www.emerson.com/en/final-control/catalog/products-and-software/valves/control-valves/control-valves-sizing. Boundary: Calculated required coefficient at operating cases differs from published rated coefficient, so operating travel and flow regime matter.

Evidence policy: no extrapolation from OPC UA to all historians; no extrapolation from Emerson valve models to all valve designs.
