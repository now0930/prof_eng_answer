## KEEP_BOUNDED (3): verified claim within the stated original conditions

- `stiction_can_induce_conditional_limit_cycles_deadband_is_not_universal`: Choudhury, Thornhill & Shah, *Modelling valve stiction*, Control Engineering Practice 13 (2005) pp.641–658, original author-hosted PDF [link](https://sites.ualberta.ca/~slshah/files/modelling_valve_stiction.pdf), abstract/introduction and § describing-function analysis: stiction may generate slip-jump and oscillations depending on loop; pure deadband/backlash does not produce a PI feedback limit cycle in the cited model **unless a plant integrator is present**. The nonexistence condition is model- and loop-specific, not general for arbitrary nonlinear networks. Publisher original PDF page 2 visually inspected.
- `control_valve_seat_contact_geometry_alignment_and_contact_width`: Fisher/Emerson *Control Valve Handbook* 5th ed (2017), §3.5–3.6, guiding/seating geometry pp.69–70 and §5.11 seat load p.112 [link](https://www.controlsouthern.com/getattachment/14bc18f1-f84e-4e5f-89eb-9cb31151946b/emerson-fisher-control-valve-handbook-fifth-edition.pdf): plug/seat guiding and sealing surface geometry affect alignment and contact; contact force divided by actual contact area governs average stress. 'Determines leakage path' is **conditional** on wear/pressure/material, not a unique leakage prediction from seat width.
- `valve_as_found_as_left_results_require_comparable_test_basis`: US NIST, SOP 26, *Gravimetric Calibration of Dynamic Volumetric Systems*, section on as-found before repairs and as-left after repairs [link](https://www.nist.gov/document/sop-26-gravimetric-calibration-dynamic-volumetric-systems-pdf); Fisher *Handbook* §5.2 defines class-specific test fluid, pressure and temperature [link](https://www.controlsouthern.com/getattachment/14bc18f1-f84e-4e5f-89eb-9cb31151946b/emerson-fisher-control-valve-handbook-fifth-edition.pdf). The measurement principle supports comparing before/after only on a comparable basis; the NIST procedure is NOT being represented as a valve-specific governing standard. Original says required records depend on project procedure.

## SOURCE_CHECKED_SCOPED_STANDARD_HOLD (1): source validated, not general scoring

- `flashing_control_valve_noise_prediction_scope_is_limited`: IEC 60534-8-4:2015 original introduction and §1 [IEC original publisher preview](https://assets.vde-verlag.de/iec-normen/preview-pdf/info_iec60534-8-4%7Bed3.0%7Db.pdf) explicitly includes liquid turbulence/cavitation and excludes flashing/unstable-flow noise; Emerson/Fisher *Control Valve Sourcebook — Pulp & Paper* (D103540X012, 2011) Ch.6 **Two-Phase Noise** [manufacturer original PDF](https://www.emerson.com/is/content/emerson/en/final-control/flow-controls/documents/d103540x012.pdf) says two-phase predictive modeling is poorly established. The Sourcebook's stronger assertion that two-phase/flashing never causes a noise problem is NOT certified as a universal physical law. This ID remains a scoped example/standard method boundary, ineligible as a universal mandatory answer fact.
