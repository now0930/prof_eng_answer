## Repair and hold requirements

- `sis_functional_safety_assessment_independent_evidence_review`: clarify FSA is technically evaluated by assessor with **appropriate degree of independence defined by risk/standard**; do not require every project evaluator external or outside project team by default.
- `functional_safety_diversity_is_ccf_measure_not_guarantee_and_has_complexity_tradeoff`: diversity can reduce CCF dependence but adds implementation tradeoffs; erase 'simple method first' as mandatory universal norm absent direct publisher evidence.
- `sis_random_hardware_failure_degradation_definition`: differentiate random hardware failure's unpredictable *time of occurrence* from physical mechanism (degradation may be one), rather than equating every random hardware failure with gradual wear.
- Retain source-based score holds for `iec_61508_tool_qualification_considers_safety_impact_and_error_detection`, `sis_control_system_validation_precedes_overall_validation`, `proven_in_use_software_requires_versioned_scope_profile_and_fault_history`: public original documentation does not establish all exact normative qualifiers; **no automatic confidence promotion**.
