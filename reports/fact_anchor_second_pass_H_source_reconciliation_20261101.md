| `sound_power_level_is_logarithmic_source_power_quantity` | KEEP_BOUNDED | OSHA, *Technical Manual* Section III Ch. 5, Appendix B, Sound Power Level, https://www.osha.gov/otm/section-3-health-hazards/chapter-5 . The publisher defines `Lw=10 log10(W/Wref)`; canonical says acoustic-power ratio uses 10log10. Reference must be specified; it is not pressure level and not electrical input power. |
| `decibel_level_is_logarithmic_and_linear_energy_must_be_combined_before_reconversion` | KEEP_BOUNDED | OSHA same chapter Appendix B.3, Combining and Averaging Sound Levels. Sound intensity/power levels require `Ltotal=10log10(sum 10^(Li/10))` under consistent basis, not arithmetic addition of dB. For pressure levels, assume incoherent/random sources or combine physical fields appropriately if coherent. |
| `sound_power_and_sound_pressure_are_not_directly_interchangeable` | KEEP_BOUNDED | US Federal Highway Administration, *Measurement of Noise* handbook, sections Sound Power, Sound Pressure and SPL, https://www.fhwa.dot.gov/Environment/noise/measurement/handbook.cfm ; OSHA Ch.5 Appendix B. Sound power describes emitted acoustic energy rate; pressure is a location/field-dependent quantity. Conversion needs geometry, directionality, environment and propagation assumptions. |
| `overall_and_frequency_band_acoustic_levels_are_distinct_reports` | KEEP_BOUNDED | FHWA *Measurement of Noise*, sections Spectrum and SPL, same URL. Broadband/overall level, resolved spectrum and octave-band levels convey different summaries of the same field; specify weighting, reference and bandwidth. Does not mean arithmetic sum of dB bands. |
| `control_valve_deadband_is_reversal_input_interval_without_observed_output_change` | KEEP_BOUNDED | IEC 60534-9:2007 definition reproduced in CENELEC EN 60534-9:2007, §3.2 and Fig. 1, https://standards.iteh.ai/catalog/standards/clc/4fbc7c31-a9ab-4918-8741-c1e4b3f394f1/en-60534-9-2007 ; IEC publisher scope https://webstore.iec.ch/en/publication/2477. Definition concerns the **input reversal interval** during which the identified observed output does not respond. Do not confuse with elapsed dead time; identification of chosen input/output variables is essential. No Annex or instrument-resolution numeric clause certified here. |
| `positioner_uses_valve_travel_feedback_to_regulate_actuator_pressure` | KEEP_BOUNDED | Emerson official *Valve Positioners*, What Is a Valve Positioner? / How a Valve Positioner Works, https://www.emerson.com/en/final-control/catalog/products-and-software/valve-actuator-regulator-instrumentation/valve-positioners . A pneumatic/analog positioner uses stem or shaft position feedback to modulate actuator air pressure and track the input setpoint. Particular digital arrangements may differ. |
| `control_valve_noise_source_path_receiver_accounting` | KEEP_BOUNDED | OSHA *Technical Manual*, Section III Ch. 5, V.A Engineering Controls (same OSHA URL); OSHA *Occupational Noise Exposure - Exposure & Controls*, https://www.osha.gov/noise/exposure-controls . Source control, propagation-path treatments and receiver exposure controls are distinct. Their claimed benefits cannot be counted twice in one modeled attenuation budget; that bookkeeping caution is not a universal additive attenuation formula. |
| `control_valve_shop_test_does_not_predict_field_leakage_across_service_conditions` | KEEP_BOUNDED | Fluid Controls Institute, *Why is ANSI/FCI 70-2 important?*, visually inspected one-page official publication, https://www.fluidcontrolsinstitute.org/70_2_InformationalSheet.pdf . It expressly describes allowable seat leakage under **defined test conditions** and in-service changes. FCI 70-2:2021 publisher-quoted scope and limitations state production testing cannot predict leakage beyond specified §5 conditions, https://standards.globalspec.com/std/14364900/fci-70-2 . Do not equate the production test rating to a guaranteed in-service leakage rate. |
| `control_valve_local_concentration_is_not_mass_emission_rate` | KEEP_BOUNDED | EPA Method 21 *Determination of Volatile Organic Compound Leaks*, §2.1, statutory text reproduced at https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/40/appendix-A-7_to_part_60 ; EPA original plain-text manuscript *Manual for Coordination of VOC Emissions Testing*, §5.2, https://nepis.epa.gov/Exe/ZyPURL.cgi?Dockey=900C0S00.TXT . Portable screening gives local concentration/locate-classify results, not direct mass emissions per time. Method 21 itself is US-regulatory-scoped and is NOT a universal obligation. |
| `fugitive_screening_and_bagging_require_distinct_measurement_setups` | KEEP_BOUNDED | EPA National Risk Assessment Research Laboratory, *Evaluation of the High Volume Collection System (HVCS) for Quantifying Fugitive Organic Vapor Leaks*, Introduction, https://nepis.epa.gov/Exe/ZyPURL.cgi?Dockey=P1000I6U.TXT . It distinguishes Method 21 portable leak screening from direct mass emission measurement via isolation/tenting and bagging with known carrier-gas flow and concentration (or a calibrated high-volume collection alternative). This is a measurement-method distinction, not a requirement to bag every leak. |
| `iec_seat_leakage_and_fugitive_packing_acceptance_are_distinct_tests` | KEEP_BOUNDED | FCI one-page official description above distinguishes internal seat leakage under fixed production conditions; EPA Method 21 §1–2 documents external component/packing fugitive-leak screening and separate instrument method. Different leak paths/criteria cannot be merged into one acceptance. Do not infer IEC 60534/ISO 15848 numerical acceptance classes or claim Method 21 alone measures mass emission. |
