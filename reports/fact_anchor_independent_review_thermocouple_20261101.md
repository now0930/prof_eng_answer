**Reviewer boundary:** CJC assumes terminal reference sensor placement and near-isothermal copper junctions. Type-specific E–T tables don't confer calibration accuracy automatically. The statement about Seebeck voltage is a simplified homogeneous-wire model; emf distribution under temperature gradient remains explicit. Extension and compensating leads must match thermoelectric properties within specified temperature limits. No hidden arbitrary 'correct' wire colors, numerical sensitivities or particular amplifier required.
